How it works

From idea to funded
in four steps.

01
Connect MetaMask
Install MetaMask, connect your wallet, and request test ETH. No KYC required. Sepolia testnet added automatically. Read the guide.
02
Browse Campaigns
Explore verified campaigns from builders across Africa. Filter by sector, country, and funding stage.
03
Fund via Escrow
Your pledge locks in a smart contract. Funds release only when milestones are DAO-verified. Full refund if the goal is not met.
04
DAO Verification
Token holders vote on milestone completions. Approved milestones trigger automatic tranche release. Full on-chain transparency.
